2. **Ad Set Level:**
   - **Locations:** Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman.
   - **Age:** 22 – 45.
   - **Gender:** Men.
   - **Languages:** English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic.
   - **Detailed Targeting:**
     - Demographics: *Lived in Pakistan (Expats)*, *Lived in India (Expats)*, *Lived in Bangladesh (Expats)*.
     - Interests: *Solo travel*, *Tourism in Dubai*, *Friendship*, *Online dating*.
   - **Placements:** Advantage+ Placements (Feed, Stories, Reels).

3. **Ad Level:**
   - Upload the 3 images in 3 different ads within the same ad set.
   - Paste the respective Primary Text, Headline, and Website URL (`https://cityhost.live`).
   - Click **Publish**!
