# City Host

**Last updated:** 2026-09-18

City Host is the product name (previously Heartlink). It is a trip-sharing site, not a dating app and not a separate “Travel Partner” brand.

## How it works

1. A traveller shares an upcoming trip on City Host (city, dates, a short note).
2. Ads send locals in that city to City Host.
3. The local finds who is visiting and messages them on this site.

Locals do not get pushed off-site to start the chat. Contact details are captured later so a reply can still reach them.

## What we do not claim

- No dating / marriage framing.
- No paid hosting, tips, or earning copy on the site.
- Ads talk about the trip and the city, not about the viewer’s personal attributes.
